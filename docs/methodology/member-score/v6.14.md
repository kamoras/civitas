# Member score v6.14

## Legislative Effectiveness follows Volden & Wiseman's LES

The full study, with every number reproduced by
`backend/scripts/research_les_stage_weighting.py`, is
[`docs/research/les-stage-weighting.md`](../../research/les-stage-weighting.md).
This record summarizes the decisions.

**Stage normalization.** Each stage's weighted bill count (introduced,
committee action, passed the chamber, became law) is now divided by the
chamber's weighted total at that stage, as in Volden & Wiseman (2014). Before,
a bill earned its significance weight times the number of stages it reached,
so a law counted as four introductions. Under V&W it counts as about 47
introductions in the House and 67 in the Senate. Against V&W's published
scores for the 110th–118th Congresses, rank correlation rose from 0.710 to
0.895 (House) and from 0.763 to 0.963 (Senate). The majority/minority median
gap stays at zero.

**No shrinkage by bill count.** A member's sponsored bills are the whole
record, not a sample, so there is no uncertainty to shrink toward 50. The
confidence grade still reports how many bills back the number. A member with
no substantive bills after half a year in office scores as a zero record. A
failed bill download stays neutral.

**Commemorative bills count 1×** against 5× for substantive bills, as in
V&W. They are recognised from the title by embedding similarity
(`analyze/commemorative.py`), with the threshold fitted by
`scripts/calibrate_commemorative.py` against V&W's 118th-Congress counts.

**Still different from V&W.** Their 10× tier (CQ Almanac major legislation)
has no source available here, and committee action is one stage, not two.
