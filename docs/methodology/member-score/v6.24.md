# Member score v6.24

## Funding Independence: the Senate's small-donor baseline is measured each run

**Before (v6.23).** A senator's small-donor share (10/53 of Funding
Independence) is compared with what their state's size predicts:
`expected = A + B·ln(state population)`, saturating at a fixed number of
points either side. A, B, the saturation and the clamp were a regression
fitted once, by `scripts/fetch_state_small_donor_baseline.py`, over 101
senators' shares, and shipped as `app/data/small_donor_baseline.json`. The
House had already moved to a reference measured every run
(`compute_funding_reference`).

**What went wrong.** The Senate the fit described is not the one being
scored. Funding is now measured on each member's most recent completed
election, over contributions (AGENTS.md §6), and the population changes
with every election. On 2026-09-29 the 100 senators' live breakdowns
(`/api/politicians/{id}`) gave a mean small-donor share of 23.2%, against
the 18.6% the file was fitted on. Every senator was compared with a
baseline about four points too low. The component, which is built to centre
on the typical senator, averaged 58.3 rather than about 50. It was a frozen
calculated constant, the pattern AGENTS.md §3a rules out.

**Now.** `compute_funding_reference` fits the same regression, by the same
method, over the senators it is about to score
(`small_donor_baseline_fit`: least squares on ln(population), saturation at
1.5 residual standard deviations, clamp at the fitted range padded 2 points).
The fit is stored in the Senate's funding reference (`small_donor_fit`), so
the API's breakdowns read the run's numbers. With fewer than 30 senators
with funding, the last stored fit is kept. The bundled file is only the
fallback before a deployment's first run. The House is unchanged: it is
compared with its own median share, since districts are apportioned by
population.

**Effect.** Recomputed from the 2026-09-29 live breakdowns. The fit moved
from 12.26 + 4.53·ln(pop) (saturation 21.2) to 16.51 + 4.77·ln(pop)
(saturation 22.2). The component now averages 48.9. 93 senators move down
and 7 are unchanged, because they were already at full credit under both
fits. Funding Independence moves by −1.8 points on average and at most
−2.6. The overall score moves by −0.6 on average and at most −0.84.
