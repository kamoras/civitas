# Member score v6.36

Funding Independence reads the small-donor share to two decimals rather
than as a whole percent.

## What changed

`normalize_finance` stored a member's small-donor share (unitemized
individual contributions over all contributions) rounded to a whole
percent. The score, the chamber references measured from it (the House
median and 10th to 90th percentile spread, the Senate's fit by state
population) and every page then read that rounded figure. A share under
0.5% was stored as 0, so a campaign with real small gifts read "0%" of the
money, past the "under 1%" display rule meant to stop that claim. The
share is now stored to two decimals, and every page states it through one
rule (`score_display.shown_share`: a whole percent rounded half up, "under
1%" above zero).

A campaign that itemizes every gift still has no small-donor share (left
out of the score, as since v6.31). Nothing else in the method changes.

## Score effect

Bounded from the formula, not replayed. Rounding moved a member's share by
at most half a point, and the reference it is compared with (the House
median, the Senate's expected share for the state) by at most half a point
too, so the gap the part scores moved by at most one point.

| Chamber | Small-donor part, points per share point | Largest move of the part | Largest move of Funding Independence |
|---|---|---|---|
| House | 50 / (p90 - p10) = 50 / 26.7 = 1.87 | 1.9 | 0.36 (0.62 when the part carries a third of the weight) |
| Senate | 50 / saturation = 50 / 22.3 = 2.25 | 2.2 | 0.42 (0.75 likewise) |

Spread and saturation are the bundled references' (`funding_reference.json`);
the spread itself moves by under one point of 26.7. The part's weight is
10/53 when Funding Independence's four parts are all measured, and at most
10/30 when parts are not. So no Funding Independence score moves by more
than about 0.75 points, and the overall score by at most about 0.25. A
shown whole number moves only where a score sat near a rounding boundary.
The 25 members whose stored share was a false 0 (real gifts under 0.5%,
measured on the live site in October 2026) are the ones whose page wording
changes.

The version changes so a shift this small is read as a method change by
the same-method calibration check (`score_calibration.SAME_METHOD_*`) and
marked on the score trend, not mistaken for a data problem.
