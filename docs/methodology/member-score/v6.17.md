# Member score v6.17

## Legislative Effectiveness counts Volden & Wiseman's fifth stage

The study is [`docs/research/les-stage-weighting.md`](../../research/les-stage-weighting.md)
(decision 5); every number is printed by
`backend/scripts/research_les_stage_weighting.py`.

**Before (v6.16).** Four stages: introduced, committee action, passed the
chamber, became law. A bill reported out of committee was classified
IN_COMMITTEE, the same as one that only had a hearing, and a bill under floor
debate (ON_FLOOR) was credited as committee action.

**Now.** Five stages, as in Volden & Wiseman (2014): introduced, action in
committee, action beyond committee, passed the chamber, became law.

- A new bill stage, **REPORTED** ("Reported by Committee"), in
  `bill_stage.py`: the House's Union Calendar code (H12410), a Discharge
  action, or a Committee or Calendars action saying reported, discharged or
  placed on a calendar. "Ordered to be reported", the markup vote, stays
  IN_COMMITTEE.
- A House subcommittee referral ("Referred to the Subcommittee on
  Health.", typed "Committee" with no action code) is REFERRED, not
  IN_COMMITTEE. It is as automatic as the first referral, and reading it as
  committee action more than tripled the House's count of bills with action
  in committee (4,996 against V&W's 1,405 for the 118th Congress; now 1,562).
- REPORTED and ON_FLOOR are LES stage 3 (`_LES_STAGE_ORDER`); passing a
  chamber is 4 and law is 5 (`_LES_MAX_STAGE`). Credit is scaled by N/5.
- On the sponsored-bills summary, a reported bill counts as advancing.

**Evidence.** Against V&W's published LES for the 110th–118th Congresses,
Spearman rank correlation within each congress and majority/minority group:

| | four stages | five stages |
|---|---|---|
| House, stage-normalized + commemorative (median / min) | 0.981 / 0.962 | 0.985 / 0.973 |
| Senate, stage-normalized + commemorative (median / min) | 0.984 / 0.966 | 0.992 / 0.974 |
| House, whole scorer (median / min) | 0.895 / 0.814 | 0.898 / 0.817 |
| Senate, whole scorer (median / min) | 0.963 / 0.863 | 0.973 / 0.873 |

The majority − minority median score gap stays zero.

The classifier itself, against V&W's per-member counts for the 118th Congress
(`scripts/research_les_stage_classifier.py`): action beyond committee now
correlates 0.936 (House) and 0.979 (Senate), from 0.806 and 0.781; House
action in committee 0.944, from 0.566.

**Rollout.** A persisted reference with four stage totals is re-measured
and stored scores rescored on first startup (`les_rescore.py`). Stored bills
keep their old stage until the nightly pipeline, or the bill-status refresh
for a bill that moves, re-derives it from its actions; scores are fully
five-stage from the first nightly run after release.
