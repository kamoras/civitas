# Member score v6.37

Two hand-set classifier checks are measured against labels the sources
themselves supply, and closed: both mostly fired on the wrong cases.

## What changed

**The procedural-bill check** (`bill_analyzer._is_procedural_seed_match`)
called a bill PROCEDURAL, which takes it out of the policy analysis, when its
title's cosine to a prototype of ceremonial and administrative bills reached
0.74. The threshold had been set by hand from a score distribution.
`scripts/calibrate_procedural.py` labels bills by the policy area the
Congressional Research Service assigns every bill, using the translation in
`app/data/crs_policy_areas.json`:

- Commemorations is procedural;
- an area that names a policy subject is not;
- the mixed areas (Congress, which holds chamber rules beside ethics and
  stock-trading bills, and Government Operations and Politics) are left out.

On the 39,967 labelled bills of a 2026-10-18 production snapshot (566
Commemorations), measured at that mix and on the held-out half:

- At 0.74 the check called **5,930 of the 39,401 substantive bills (15%)**
  procedural, and was **4.3% precise**.
- No threshold reaches the Action Center's precision rule (an exact lower
  95% bound of at least 0.80). The best is 8.5% precise.

The check is closed. Bills reach a policy area through the reference kNN and
the prototypes, as every other bill does. A title under five characters is
still procedural.

**The payment-processor check's embedding tier** (`is_skip_entity`,
`skip_entities_batch`) dropped a donor whose name was close to a prototype of
ActBlue, WinRed and Anedot, at 0.67, also set by hand.
`scripts/calibrate_donor_gates.py` labels names from the FEC's own receipts:

- a conduit whose every own receipt is a memo entry is a processor;
- a committee that is never a conduit and has no memo receipt is not.

Two processors exist beyond the keyword list's names, against 10,560
committees. At 0.67 the tier passed **31 held-out names, none a processor,
and skipped 64 real committees** in all. It is closed, and the keyword tier
(ActBlue, WinRed, Anedot, ...) decides.

The same script measures four other donor gates and records each in
`app/data/donor_gate_calibration.json`, with the reason it is not applied
yet. Their thresholds are still the hand-set ones:

| Gate | Hand-set value, held out | Best fit | Why not applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| Donor type (semantic tier) | 0.60: 24% right | no threshold meets the rule | its fallback's accuracy is unmeasured |
| Individual-name skip | 0.75: 61% precise | no threshold meets the rule | which error a page should show |
| Employer not an organization | 0.78 | no threshold meets the rule | the labels miss status values (SELF-EMPLOYED) |
| Transfer memo | 0.78: 76% precise, 30% recall | 0.82: 92% precise, 9% recall | trades one counting error for another |

## Score effect

Not replayed.

- **Partisan depth by policy area** moves where a member's votes include
  bills the procedural check had set aside.
- **Funding Independence and Funding Diversity** move where a member's
  donors include a committee the processor tier had skipped.

The analysis-code fingerprint changes, so the first run after release
classifies every bill and donor afresh.
