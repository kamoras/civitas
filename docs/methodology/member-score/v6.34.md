# Member score v6.34

Data corrections in four places, each measured against a source before it
shipped. Two change scores.

## A donor's industry from its neighbours only when they agree

A donor name the industry prototypes can't place was given the plurality
label of its seven nearest classified names (kNN). Every one of the seven
counted: the call passed a similarity floor of 0.20, under the documented
0.65.

That vote was measured on 177 PAC sponsors whose industry the records
state (a labor organization's FEC type, a corporation's SEC industry code)
and which the prototype tier abstains on:

| Rule | Answered | Right |
|---|---|---|
| plurality, any floor from 0.20 to 0.80 | 177 | 29% |
| 6 of 7 neighbours agree | 14 | 10 |
| all 7 agree | 8 | 8 |

The floor makes no difference: a name's nearest classified name scores
above 0.81 in nine cases of ten whatever its industry. The industry is
now taken from kNN only when all seven neighbours agree; otherwise the
donor stays unclassified, which no industry total counts.

In the production learning store (2026-10-09), 7,115 of the 7,274
industries kNN had assigned become unclassified: 1,333 law firms, 1,151
lobbying firms, 653 finance, 638 technology among them.

The donor-type kNN beside it was measured the same way, on 300 names the
FEC's committee file and the lobbying registry label: the plurality of
five is right 76% of the time at every floor from 0.25 to 0.75, and a
stricter vote lowers that, because a type kNN can't settle falls back to
Org/Employees. It keeps the plurality and now uses the documented floor.

**Effect on Funding Diversity**, re-scored on a copy of production taken
2026-10-09 (each member's stored breakdown with the committee money of
their listed donors moved off the industries kNN gave them; the before
scores reproduce all 533 stored scores):

- 324 of 533 members move: 220 by -1, 62 by -2, 26 by -3, 7 by -4, 3 by -5,
  3 by -6, and 3 by +1.
- Industry money that is no longer counted lowers the classified share,
  which counts toward source breadth at a higher weight than unclassified
  money.

## One employer typed two ways is one donor

Donors type their employer, and the FEC's employer totals keep each
spelling apart ("ASSOCIATES" / "ASSCOIATES"), splitting one employer's
employees into two donors. Spellings that differ only in punctuation, or in
one word misspelled, are now merged (`normalize_finance.
merge_misspelled_employers`).

Calibrated on the employer totals cached for 654 committees (70,744 names):
829 names were another's spelling but for punctuation; of 95,409 pairs one
word apart, 168 sampled across the similarity bands were judged by hand.
At a word similarity of 0.80, both spellings five letters or longer and no
digits, the merge is right on an estimated 95.5% of the pairs it joins and
finds 78.7% of the one-word misspellings. Its known wrong joins are
near-namesakes (two pairs of universities named for a compass direction).

**Effect on Funding Independence** (the top-ten donors' share), re-scored
on the same copy from each member's stored donor list: 85 members have a
merged employer, the top-ten share changes for 58, and the score moves for
21: 19 by -1, one by -2, one by -3. The pipeline merges before the list is
cut to 100, so it can find a few more.

## An independent representative's caucus

A House independent is now read with the caucus the Clerk records
(`<caucus>` in MemberData.xml). It was inferred from votes, and the
inference had given one independent no caucus and so no party-line
record. The Clerk records that member in the Republican caucus; the member
chairs a subcommittee, a seat the majority conference assigns. The Senate
publishes no such field, so senators are still inferred.

**Effect on Constituent Alignment**, re-scored on the same copy (the
before scores reproduce all 433 stored House scores): the member's record
is now 295 party-labeled votes with 29 breaks, and the score moves from 50
to 30. With one more Republican in the House's Republican expectation fit,
56 other representatives move by one to four points (12 by -1, 31 by +1, 7
by +2, 5 by +3, 1 by +4).

## Not changed: a credit-union trade group read as labor unions

Adding "credit union league association of credit unions" to the FINANCE
prototype moved the group off LABOR_UNIONS (5 of 25 credit-union PACs read
as unions, 1 after). But it relabelled 434 of the learning store's 25,403
names, and of 60 judged by hand 19 improved and 19 got worse. It was not
kept.
